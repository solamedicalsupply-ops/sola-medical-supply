Why Expiry Date and Batch Number Matter

Understanding expiry dates and batch numbers in aesthetic products is crucial for buyers. Learn why they matter and how to verify them.

SOLA Knowledge Team · July 05, 2026

Expiry dates and batch numbers are critical for ensuring the safety and efficacy of aesthetic products. This article explores their importance in procurement.

In the fast-paced world of aesthetic products, ensuring the safety and efficacy of your purchases is paramount. Buyers often face challenges in verifying the quality of products, especially when it comes to expiry dates and batch numbers. Understanding these elements can significantly impact the quality of services provided in clinics, spas, and other establishments.

What is it?

The expiry date and batch number are critical identifiers for any aesthetic product. The expiry date indicates the final date on which the product is guaranteed to retain its intended efficacy and safety when stored under recommended conditions. After this date, the product may degrade, leading to reduced effectiveness or potential safety risks.

The batch number, on the other hand, is a unique identifier assigned to a specific production run of a product. It helps in tracing the product back to its manufacturing details, including the date of production, the ingredients used, and the quality control measures in place. This information is essential for both regulatory compliance and consumer safety.

Who usually orders it?

Typically, aesthetic products are ordered by a variety of professionals in the beauty and healthcare sectors. This includes:

  • Clinics: Medical professionals who administer aesthetic treatments often require products with clear expiry dates and batch numbers to ensure patient safety.
  • Spas: Spa owners and managers need to verify that the products they use are fresh and safe for their clients.
  • Resellers and Distributors: Those who distribute aesthetic products must ensure that their stock is compliant and safe for resale.

Each of these buyers has a vested interest in the quality and safety of the products they procure, making the verification of expiry dates and batch numbers essential.

What buyers should check

When procuring aesthetic products, buyers should be vigilant about checking the following:

  • Expiry Date: Always verify that the product's expiry date is clearly labeled and that it has not passed. Products that are past their expiry date should not be used.
  • Batch Number: Ensure that each product has a batch number for traceability. This is important for tracking any issues that may arise post-purchase.
  • Storage Instructions: Check if there are specific storage conditions that need to be adhered to in order to maintain the product's integrity.
  • Documentation: Request any relevant documentation from the supplier that confirms the product’s compliance with safety standards.

By meticulously checking these details, buyers can significantly reduce the risk of using compromised products.

Shipping / packing / storage notes

Proper shipping, packing, and storage practices are vital for maintaining the quality of aesthetic products. Here are some key considerations:

  • Temperature Control: Many aesthetic products are sensitive to temperature changes. Ensure that products are shipped in temperature-controlled environments, especially if they require refrigeration.
  • Protective Packaging: Use protective packaging materials to prevent damage during transit. This includes cushioning materials and temperature-resistant containers.
  • Clear Labeling: Packages should be clearly labeled with handling instructions, including any temperature requirements and expiry dates.
  • Storage Conditions: Once received, products should be stored according to the manufacturer's recommendations. This often includes keeping them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Adhering to these guidelines ensures that the products remain effective and safe for use.

MOQ / quotation / how to order

Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and pricing can vary significantly depending on the supplier and the type of aesthetic products being ordered. Here are some steps to follow:

  • Determine Your Needs: Assess your clinic or spa's requirements to understand how much stock you will need.
  • Request a Quotation: Contact suppliers to request a quotation based on your desired quantity. Make sure to inquire about any bulk discounts that may be available.
  • Check MOQ: Ensure that the order meets the supplier's MOQ requirements to avoid issues with your order.
  • Place Your Order: Once you have received and reviewed the quotation, proceed with placing your order while keeping track of the expiry dates and batch numbers.

Efficient order management helps in maintaining a steady supply of products while ensuring quality and safety.

FAQ

Why are expiry dates important for aesthetic products?

Expiry dates are crucial as they indicate the timeframe within which a product is guaranteed to be effective and safe. Using products past their expiry can lead to adverse effects.

How can I verify the batch number?

Batch numbers can typically be verified by contacting the manufacturer or supplier. They can provide details about the production run and any associated quality control measures.

What should I do if I receive expired products?

If you receive expired products, contact your supplier immediately to report the issue and request a replacement or refund.

Are there specific storage conditions for all aesthetic products?

Storage conditions can vary by product. Always refer to the manufacturer's guidelines for specific storage requirements to ensure product integrity.

How can I ensure I am ordering from a reputable supplier?

Research potential suppliers, check for customer reviews, and ask for documentation that confirms their compliance with safety standards before placing an order.
