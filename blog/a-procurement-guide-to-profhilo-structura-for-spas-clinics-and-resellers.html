How to compare options before ordering

When evaluating suppliers for Profhilo Structura, consider the following criteria:

  • Product Type: Ensure you are sourcing the correct variant of Profhilo Structura that aligns with your treatment goals.
  • Brand Reputation: Research the supplier’s reputation in the market for reliability and quality. Look for reviews and testimonials.
  • Packaging: Check if the product packaging meets your business needs, including sizes and quantities that suit your clientele.
  • Batch/Expiry Visibility: Ensure suppliers provide clear information on batch numbers and expiry dates for traceability and compliance.
  • Supplier Communication: Evaluate how responsive and informative the supplier is during the inquiry process; good communication can prevent future issues.
  • Destination Requirements: Confirm that the supplier can meet your shipping needs based on your location and any specific delivery requirements.

Shipping, packing and documentation questions

Logistics is a critical aspect of procurement. Here are some common questions buyers may have:

  • What are the shipping options available? Suppliers typically offer various shipping methods; confirm which is best for your needs, considering cost and speed.
  • How is the product packed? Ensure that the packing meets safety standards to prevent damage during transit and maintain product integrity.
  • What documentation is required? Confirm what paperwork is needed for customs and local regulations to ensure compliance and avoid delays.
  • How do I track my shipment? Ask suppliers about their tracking systems to monitor your order and stay informed about delivery status.
  • What happens if there is a shipping delay? Understand the supplier’s policy on delays and how they communicate with buyers to manage expectations.
