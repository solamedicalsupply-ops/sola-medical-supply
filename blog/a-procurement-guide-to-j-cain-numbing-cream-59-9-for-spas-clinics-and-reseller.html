Shipping, packing and documentation questions

Logistics play a crucial role in the procurement process. Here are some common questions to consider:

  • What are the shipping options available? Understand the different shipping methods and their associated costs to choose the best option for your needs.
  • What packaging standards are followed? Ensure that the product is packaged securely to prevent damage during transit and complies with industry standards.
  • What documentation is required for shipping? Familiarize yourself with any necessary import/export documentation to avoid delays and ensure compliance with regulations.
  • Are there any customs considerations? Check if there are specific customs regulations that may affect your shipment, including duties and taxes.

MOQ, quotation and reorder planning

When planning your orders, consider the following:

  • Determine the minimum order quantity (MOQ) set by the supplier to ensure you meet their requirements and avoid delays.
  • Prepare a forecast of your anticipated demand to avoid stockouts and ensure you have adequate inventory on hand.
  • Consider any product variants you may need to order, such as different packaging sizes or formulations.
  • Communicate your destination details clearly to the supplier to ensure accurate shipping and delivery.
