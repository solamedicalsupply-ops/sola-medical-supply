How to compare options before ordering

When comparing wholesale suppliers for GTM 8% products, consider the following criteria:

  • Product Type: Ensure you are sourcing the correct formulation that meets your clinic's needs. Different formulations may cater to specific skin types or treatment goals.
  • Brand Reputation: Research suppliers with a strong reputation in the aesthetic industry. Look for reviews and testimonials from other clinics to gauge reliability.
  • Packaging: Look for suppliers that provide secure and compliant packaging to maintain product integrity. Proper packaging can prevent contamination and ensure the product remains effective.
  • Batch/Expiry Visibility: Ensure that suppliers provide clear information on batch numbers and expiry dates. This transparency is crucial for maintaining compliance and ensuring product safety.
  • Supplier Communication: Assess how responsive and informative the supplier is during the inquiry process. Good communication can prevent misunderstandings and streamline the ordering process.
  • Destination Requirements: Confirm that the supplier can meet your shipping and delivery needs based on your location. Some suppliers may have restrictions on shipping certain products to specific regions.

Buyer checklist before requesting a quote

  • Define your product requirements (type, quantity, and variants).
  • Research potential wholesale suppliers and assess their credibility.
  • Prepare documentation requirements for your order, including any necessary certifications.
  • Verify shipping options and costs associated with your order.
  • Establish your budget and pricing expectations.
  • Clarify your timeline for delivery and reorder planning.
  • Consider potential storage requirements for the products upon arrival.

Shipping, packing and documentation questions

Logistics play a critical role in the sourcing process. When ordering GTM 8% products, consider the following:

  • What are the shipping options available, and how do they affect delivery times? Different carriers may offer varying speeds and costs.
  • What packing methods do suppliers use to prevent damage during transit? Ensure that products are well-protected to avoid losses.
  • What documentation is required for customs clearance, if applicable? Be aware of any import regulations that may apply to your orders.
  • How will you track your shipment once it has been dispatched? Ask suppliers about tracking options for peace of mind.
  • What are the return policies in case of damaged or incorrect items? Knowing this can save you from potential losses.

MOQ, quotation and reorder planning
