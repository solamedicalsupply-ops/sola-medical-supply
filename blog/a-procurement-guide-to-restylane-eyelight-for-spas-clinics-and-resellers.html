How to compare options before ordering

When evaluating potential suppliers for Restylane Eyelight, consider the following criteria:

  • Product Type: Ensure the product meets your specific needs and client expectations, including formulation and intended use.
  • Brand Reputation: Research the supplier's history and customer feedback to gauge reliability and product quality.
  • Packaging: Assess whether the packaging protects the product during transit and storage, maintaining sterility and integrity.
  • Batch and Expiry Visibility: Confirm that the supplier provides clear information on batch numbers and expiration dates to ensure product safety.
  • Supplier Communication: Evaluate how responsive and informative the supplier is during the inquiry process, as good communication can prevent future issues.
  • Destination Requirements: Understand any specific shipping requirements for your location, including customs regulations and potential fees.

Buyer checklist before requesting a quote

  • Confirm product specifications and variants needed, including syringe sizes and quantities.
  • Determine your minimum order quantity (MOQ) based on your business needs.
  • Verify shipping destination and any special requirements, such as temperature control.
  • Gather necessary documentation for importation, including business licenses and tax IDs.
  • Prepare questions regarding lead times, payment terms, and return policies.
  • Review supplier return policies and warranty information to protect your investment.

Shipping, packing and documentation questions

Logistics play a crucial role in the procurement process. Here are some common questions buyers may have:

  • What are the shipping options available? Different suppliers may offer various shipping methods, including express and standard shipping, so evaluate which best suits your needs.
  • How is the product packaged for shipping? Ensure that the supplier uses appropriate packaging to prevent damage during transit, especially for sensitive products like dermal fillers.
  • What documentation is required for customs clearance? Familiarize yourself with the necessary paperwork, such as invoices, import permits, and any health certifications required by your country.
  • How can I track my order? Ask suppliers about their tracking systems to monitor shipment progress and ensure timely delivery.
  • What are the potential customs fees? Be aware of any additional costs that may arise during the import process, as these can impact your overall budget.
