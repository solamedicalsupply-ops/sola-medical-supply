How to compare options before ordering

When evaluating potential suppliers for Rejuran I, consider the following comparison criteria:

  • Product Type: Ensure the supplier offers the specific variant of Rejuran I that meets your needs, as different formulations may target various skin concerns.
  • Brand Reputation: Research the supplier's reputation in the market and their history of reliability. Look for testimonials or case studies that highlight their service quality.
  • Packaging: Assess the packaging for safety and ease of use, as well as the visibility of batch and expiry dates. Proper packaging ensures product integrity during transit.
  • Supplier Communication: Effective communication is key; ensure the supplier is responsive and provides clear information regarding product specifications and order status.
  • Destination Requirements: Confirm that the supplier can meet your shipping and delivery requirements based on your location, including any specific regulations that may apply.

Shipping, packing and documentation questions

MOQ, quotation and reorder planning

Understanding minimum order quantities (MOQ) is essential for effective procurement. Different suppliers may have varying MOQs, which can impact your purchasing strategy. When preparing to order, consider the following:

  • Determine the quantities you need based on your client demand and inventory turnover, ensuring you have enough stock to meet peak times.
  • Identify any product variants you wish to stock, as different formulations may be necessary for diverse client needs.
