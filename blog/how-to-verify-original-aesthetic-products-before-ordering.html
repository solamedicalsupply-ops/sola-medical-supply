How to Verify Original Aesthetic Products Before Ordering

Learn how to verify original aesthetic products before ordering. Essential tips for clinics, spas, resellers, and distributors.

SOLA Knowledge Team · July 03, 2026

Ensure the authenticity of aesthetic products before making a purchase. This guide provides essential tips for clinics, spas, and distributors.

In the world of aesthetic medicine, the authenticity of products is paramount. Buyers often face challenges in verifying the originality of aesthetic products before placing orders. This guide aims to equip you with the necessary knowledge to navigate these challenges effectively.

What is it?

Aesthetic products encompass a wide range of items used in cosmetic procedures, including injectables, dermal fillers, and skincare treatments. These products are designed to enhance appearance and promote skin health. However, the market is saturated with counterfeit and substandard products that can pose serious risks to patients and damage the reputation of clinics and spas. Therefore, understanding how to verify original aesthetic products before ordering is crucial for ensuring safety and efficacy.

Who usually orders it?

The primary buyers of aesthetic products include:

  • Clinics: Medical facilities offering cosmetic treatments and procedures.
  • Spas: Wellness centers that provide aesthetic services, often incorporating skincare and beauty treatments.
  • Resellers: Distributors who purchase products in bulk to sell to clinics and spas.
  • Distributors: Companies that manage the supply chain of aesthetic products, ensuring they reach end-users.

These buyers must prioritize the verification of product authenticity to maintain patient safety and uphold their business integrity.

What buyers should check

When considering an order for aesthetic products, buyers should perform thorough checks to confirm their authenticity. Here are key factors to consider:

  • Supplier Reputation: Research the supplier’s history and reviews. A reputable supplier will have positive feedback and a solid track record.
  • Product Packaging: Examine the packaging for quality. Original products typically have high-quality packaging, including security seals and holograms.
  • Batch Numbers and Expiry Dates: Verify batch numbers and expiry dates on the product packaging. Authentic products will have clear and legible information.
  • Certificates of Authenticity: Request certificates or documents that verify the product's authenticity from the manufacturer.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Ensure that the product complies with local regulations and standards. This can often be confirmed through regulatory bodies.

By conducting these checks, buyers can significantly reduce the risk of acquiring counterfeit products.

Shipping / packing / storage notes

Proper shipping, packing, and storage of aesthetic products are essential for maintaining their integrity. Here are some considerations:

  • Temperature Control: Many aesthetic products require specific temperature controls during shipping and storage. Verify that your supplier uses temperature-controlled logistics.
  • Packaging Integrity: Ensure that products are packaged securely to prevent damage during transit. Look for tamper-proof seals and protective materials.
  • Storage Conditions: Once received, products should be stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Follow the manufacturer’s guidelines for optimal storage conditions.

Adhering to these shipping and storage practices helps ensure that the products remain effective and safe for use.

MOQ / quotation / how to order

Understanding the minimum order quantity (MOQ), obtaining quotations, and knowing how to place an order are crucial steps in the procurement process:

  • Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): Different suppliers will have varying MOQs. Familiarize yourself with these requirements to plan your orders accordingly.
  • Obtaining Quotations: Reach out to suppliers for quotations based on your desired products and quantities. This will help you budget and assess pricing options.
  • How to Order: Once you have selected a supplier, follow their ordering process, which may include filling out forms, providing necessary documentation, and confirming payment methods.

Being informed about these aspects can streamline the purchasing process and prevent potential delays.

FAQ

What are the risks of buying counterfeit aesthetic products?

Counterfeit products can lead to ineffective treatments, adverse reactions, and legal issues for clinics and spas. It is essential to verify authenticity to avoid these risks.

How can I find reputable suppliers for aesthetic products?

Look for suppliers with positive reviews, industry certifications, and a proven track record. Networking within the industry can also help identify reliable sources.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious product?

If you suspect a product is counterfeit, do not use it. Contact the supplier immediately and consider reporting the issue to local authorities or regulatory bodies.

Are there specific certifications I should look for?

While specific certifications vary by region, look for compliance with local health regulations and any industry-specific quality standards.

Can I return products if they are found to be counterfeit?

Return policies vary by supplier. Ensure you review the return policy before ordering, and keep documentation of your purchase to facilitate returns if necessary.
