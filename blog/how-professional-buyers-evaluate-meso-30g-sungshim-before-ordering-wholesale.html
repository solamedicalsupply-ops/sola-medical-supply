How to compare options before ordering

When considering wholesale orders for Meso 30g Sungshim, buyers should establish clear comparison criteria. Here are some practical factors to evaluate:

  • Product Type: Ensure the product matches your specific needs, including needle size and type.
  • Brand Reputation: Research the brand’s history and customer feedback to gauge reliability.
  • Packaging: Look for packaging that ensures product integrity and ease of use.
  • Batch/Expiry Visibility: Confirm that the supplier provides clear information on batch numbers and expiry dates.
  • Supplier Communication: Assess how responsive and informative the supplier is during the inquiry process.
  • Destination Requirements: Consider any specific shipping or handling requirements based on your location.

Shipping, packing and documentation questions

Logistics play a critical role in the wholesale purchasing process. Buyers should consider the following questions:

  • What are the estimated shipping costs based on the order size and destination?
  • How does the supplier handle packaging to prevent damage during transit?
  • What documentation will be provided with the shipment, such as invoices and compliance certificates?
  • Are there specific customs requirements for importing this product into your region?
  • What is the expected lead time for shipping, and how are delays communicated?
